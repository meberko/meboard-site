MeBoard Support

A Gboard-style keyboard for iPhone and iPad: custom layouts, haptics, glide typing, autocorrect and a split keyboard for large screens.

Turning MeBoard on

  1. Open Settings → Apps → MeBoard → Keyboards.
  2. Turn on MeBoard.
  3. Turn on Allow Full Access for haptics and your custom settings (why this is safe).
  4. In any text field, hold the 🌐 globe key and choose MeBoard.

The MeBoard app shows a checklist that ticks off each step as you finish it.

Common questions

Haptics don't work. Haptics need Allow Full Access to be on (Settings → Apps → MeBoard → Keyboards). Then check Haptics & sound in the MeBoard app.

My settings don't show up on the keyboard. Settings reach the keyboard through Full Access, and apply the next time the keyboard opens.

Where are emoji? Emoji suggestions appear in the bar above the keys as you type. For the full emoji keyboard, hold 🌐 and choose Emoji.

How do I type a symbol or accent? Hold a key. The small character in its corner is the first option.

Autocorrect changed a word I wanted. Press backspace right after the correction to undo it, and MeBoard stops correcting that word.

The keyboard is split in two. On large screens (iPhone Duo unfolded, iPad) MeBoard can split for thumb typing. Change this under Layout & appearance → Split keyboard.

MeBoard doesn't appear in password fields. iOS always uses Apple's keyboard for passwords. That's by design.
