Privacy Policy

MeBoard keyboard for iPhone and iPad · Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: MeBoard does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. It has no network code, no analytics and no third-party services. Everything stays on your device.

What MeBoard stores, and where

To work, MeBoard keeps a small amount of information only on your device, in storage shared between the MeBoard app and its keyboard:

  • Your settings (layout, appearance, haptics, typing options) and any custom layouts you create.
  • Words you teach autocorrect, for example by undoing a correction.
  • Word pairs learned from your typing, used to predict your next word. You can turn this off with "Learn from my typing".
  • Your text shortcuts.

MeBoard never learns from email, web address or search fields. iOS never uses third-party keyboards for password fields.

None of this leaves your device, and MeBoard cannot send it anywhere. Like any app's data, it may be included in your own encrypted device backups, which Apple controls under your Apple account's privacy settings.

What MeBoard does not do

  • It does not send what you type, or anything else, over the internet.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

"Allow Full Access"

When you turn on Full Access, iOS shows a generic warning that a keyboard could transmit what you type. MeBoard asks for Full Access only because iOS requires it for two things: haptic feedback, and reading the settings you choose in the MeBoard app. MeBoard works without Full Access, but without haptics or your custom settings.

Deleting your data

In the MeBoard app, "Forget learned words & phrases" erases what MeBoard has learned. Deleting the MeBoard app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

MeBoard collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. MeBoard will not start collecting data without asking you first.
